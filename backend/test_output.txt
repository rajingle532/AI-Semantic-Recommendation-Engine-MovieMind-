"Dhurandhar" is a recent Marathi comedy-drama film that released in **2024**. Directed by the acclaimed **Kedar Shinde**, the movie brings together a stellar cast including **Ankush Chaudhari**, **Sai Tamhankar**, and **Siddharth Jadhav** in pivotal roles. It's a blend of humor and emotional depth, characteristic of Shinde's directorial style, often exploring relatable societal themes with a lighthearted touch. The film has been anticipated for its entertaining storyline and strong performances from its lead actors. 🎬🎭📅
